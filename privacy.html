Privacy policy

This policy describes the Tenfold Puzzles iOS app and related websites operated by its creator.

Game saves and backups

Puzzle progress and settings are stored on your device. Downloaded backups contain your progress and unfinished puzzle states. You choose where to save or share them. Daily challenge history, streaks, and mastery stars are also stored locally, separately from trail backups. Clearing browser data can remove these records and your local progress; backups do not sync automatically.

Progress sharing

Progress sharing is enabled by default. While it is on, the game sends a persistent app- or browser-generated identifier, sessions, puzzle and level activity, completion totals, hints, skips, and activity times to the creator’s private dashboard. You may add a nickname. We do not ask for your location or store your IP address in the game’s progress database. Hosting providers may process network information to serve and protect the site.

Turn off future progress updates under Settings → Sharing & nickname. Previously shared data remains stored. One person on multiple devices can count as multiple players.

Feedback you submit

Sending feedback shares your message, an app-generated identifier, optional nickname, puzzle/level, moves, screen size, browser details, and game version with the creator. This works independently of progress sharing. Do not include private information. Reports are visible only through the owner dashboard and retained for testing and support until removed by the creator. We do not sell feedback or progress records.

Optional ads and Google privacy choices

The browser playtest uses simulated ads. The iOS app uses Google AdMob to request rewarded ads. Watching an ad is optional and can earn one hint or one puzzle skip after the completion event; you can continue playing without requesting an ad.

The iOS app asks Google's User Messaging Platform for updated privacy information when it opens and before requesting an ad. Where a message is available and required, Google presents consent choices. Ad requests are blocked unless Google's SDK reports that ads may be requested. Allowing an ad request is not the same as consenting to personalized advertising. The iOS app requests non-personalized ads and does not prompt for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission.

Google's advertising and consent services may process device identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location, device and network information, ad interactions, consent choices, and diagnostics. Non-personalized ads can still involve processing data. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google controls retention of information it processes under its policies.

Use Settings → Ad privacy choices to reopen Google's privacy form where required, including supported U.S. state opt-out choices. These choices apply to Google's advertising services; the separate Sharing & nickname control governs playtest progress sent to the creator. A refusal or unavailable ad does not lock normal puzzle play. Consent messages must be published and reachable for the relevant app and region; if they cannot be loaded, optional ads may remain unavailable.

Public information website

This privacy and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses, to provide and protect the service. See GitHub privacy statement. These information pages do not load advertising, analytics scripts, or a feedback form.

Service providers and owner access

The website and database use Sites hosting infrastructure. Only the configured owner can access the dashboard through sign-in. Player accounts are not required. Players cannot view other players’ reports.

Your choices and contact

Use Settings for progress sharing, backup, and ad privacy choices. For questions or deletion requests, email tenfoldpuzzles@yahoo.com or visit our support page. Include your optional nickname and approximate report date so the creator can identify the relevant record; do not send passwords or identity documents. Removing server records does not remove local saves, and turning off sharing does not delete prior records.

Children’s eligibility, production advertising settings, retention practices, and store disclosures must be finalized before launch.
