Tenfold Puzzles

Help & support

For questions, feedback, or deletion requests, email tenfoldpuzzles@yahoo.com.

For a puzzle problem, include the level, puzzle type, device model, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Do not include passwords, identity documents, or payment information.

Quick answers

Each puzzle has a How to Play button. Progress is saved on the device. Use Settings → Save progress backup to export your trail before moving to another device or clearing app data.

Use the feedback option inside the game to attach puzzle details automatically. This public information site has no submission form.
